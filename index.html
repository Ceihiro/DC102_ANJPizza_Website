Our Story

Made from home, made with love

AnJ Pizza opened its doors on November 25, 2022. What started as a homemade recipe turned into a neighborhood favorite in Brgy Longos, Mataas na Kahoy, Batangas.

We make every pizza from scratch, fresh dough, real cheese, and quality toppings at prices everyone can afford.
